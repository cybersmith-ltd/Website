Defence Cyber Certification.

For suppliers to the MoD in the UK defence supply chain.

What Is It.

Defence Cyber Certification (DCC) is the Ministry of Defence’s cybersecurity certification for its supply chain. It gives suppliers a single, organisation-wide certification to present in defence contracts.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "padlock-plus" "size" 40) }}

Built on Cyber Essentials

Every level starts with Cyber Essentials. Levels 2 and 3 also require Cyber Essentials+.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "shield-chevrons" "size" 40) }}

4 Levels

Levels 0 to 3, with more controls at each level. The MoD sets the level a contract requires.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "certificate-seal" "size" 40) }}

3 Year Certificate

Certification lasts three years, with an attestation every year.
